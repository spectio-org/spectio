# F2 · Synthetic scenario pipelines on both engines

!!! success "Done 2026-10-26 · commit b2687d8 · 11 / 11 scenarios on Nextflow 26.10.3, 25.10.9 and Snakemake 9.27.1 (one scenario repeated once after a known engine fault)"

## What changed

- `tests/pipelines/synthetic/` holds two twin pipelines, one per engine:
  - `nextflow/main.nf` and `snakemake/workflow/Snakefile`;
  - the same three stages (`qc`, then a gate, `align`, then a gate, `quant`);
  - deterministic task bodies (`bin/synth.py`);
  - injected failures: exit, OOM, OOM once, timeout, flaky.
- `scenarios.yaml` lists 11 PR scenarios and 1 nightly stress scenario. `run_scenarios.py` checks:
  - the run's exit code;
  - outputs that must or must not exist;
  - per-process task counts, with their exit codes and attempts;
  - cached tasks after a resume.
- **Known engine faults.** These are listed with their log signature. A matching failure is repeated once and reported as `PASS*`.
- **Specs changed:**
  - [dev environment §10.1](../../specs/dev-environment-and-testing.md#101-synthetic-scenario-pipelines-testspipelinessynthetic): what the corpus proves;
  - [schedulers §3.5](../../specs/schedulers-and-monitoring.md#35-turning-records-into-meaning): a new failure class, and "classify from the record, not from the exit code".

## Why

Spectio's gates rely on engine mechanisms: a generated Nextflow config with `-resume`, and Snakemake `--until`. Those had to be proven on both engines before any code depends on them. See [checking scenarios](../../specs/checking-scenarios.md).

Prompt: Thomas's request for real Snakemake and Nextflow tests. Decision: [decision log, 2026-10-26, real pipelines](../../decisions/log.md#2026-10-26-real-pipelines-versioning-staging-access-and-auth-parity).

## Findings and open questions

- **Snakemake 9.27.1** drops a retried job when a checkpoint updates the DAG at the same moment ("BUG: Out of jobs ready to be started"). This happened in 4 of the 18 executions of `oom_once_retry` in this session (two full suites, loops of 8 and 6, and 2 repeats, both of which passed). A plain rerun completes.
- **Nextflow 25.10.9** reports no exit code for a local time-limit kill. 26.10.3 reports 143.
- **Not covered here:** the SLURM (sind) and Kubernetes (kind) legs need cgroup v2 Docker. They are steps T3 and T4.
