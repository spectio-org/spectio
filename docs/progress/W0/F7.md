## What changed

- **`tools/autopilot/statusline.py`:** a status line that writes plan usage to `.autopilot/usage.json`.
- **`tools/autopilot/budget.py`:**
  - two modes: a number of points of a window (percentage points of the 7-day or the 5-hour plan window, counted from the start), or until the window is used up;
  - batch sizing;
  - waiting for the 5-hour reset;
  - limit detection from the stream, and from the `StopFailure` hook once Thomas adds it ([autopilot §4](../../playbooks/autopilot.md#4-permissions-to-add-yourself));
  - calibration of percent per USD.
- **`tools/autopilot/run.sh`:** now a governed loop of headless ticks.
- **Status line:** the AUTOPILOT line shows the budget.
- **Docs:** [autopilot §6](../../playbooks/autopilot.md#6-budget-governor-a-share-of-the-weekly-window-or-until-the-quota-runs-out).

## Why

Spectio is a side project. Thomas wants to give it a share of his weekly window at any time of the week, or let it use the rest of his quota. For example: start on Wednesday at 11:00 and stop when the window resets at 15:00.

Decision: [decision log, 2026-10-26, budget governor](../../decisions/log.md#2026-10-26-documentation-as-part-of-done-telemetry-budget-governor).
