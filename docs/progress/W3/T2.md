## Findings and open questions

**Findings.** 24 differences, each with its source and the spec section it touches, are in the [note, Differences with the specs](../../research/spike-engine-events-2026-10-28.md#differences-with-the-specs). The ones that change behaviour:

1. **End of stage on Nextflow** ([checking-scenarios §11.5](../../specs/checking-scenarios.md#115-limits)): the last `onTaskComplete` of a process arrives after its `onProcessTerminate`, in every run, on both versions.
2. **Attempt state in `nf-spectio`:** a failed attempt has `handler.status` `COMPLETED`; the failure is in the trace record and `errorAction`.
3. **Flow end:** `onFlowError` arrives after `onFlowComplete`, and 25.10.9 sent `onFlowComplete` twice in a failed run.
4. **Snakemake exit codes** are only in the text of the `error` record, never in `job_error`.
5. **`nf-tower` is downloaded** from the plugin registry on first use, not shipped in the `-dist` jar: a constraint for offline clusters, and for `nf-spectio` too.

**Open questions,** for TRIAGE3:

- Should `nf-spectio` send the task `script` and `env` (the Tower client does)?
- Is end of stage "all processes terminated and all their tasks completed", or a short drain after the last `onProcessTerminate`?

**Not done:** a Gradle build and registry publication of `nf-spectio`, transport behaviour when the server is down, `/heartbeat`, Nextflow 25.04.8. They are follow-ups in the [note](../../research/spike-engine-events-2026-10-28.md#follow-ups).
