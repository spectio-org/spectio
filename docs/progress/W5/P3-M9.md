### FU-P3-53 rule 10 (b)

Thomas, 2026-11-08, chose option (b) of [inbox #116](../../reviews/inbox.md#116-assumed-process-states-outputs-slurm-request-and-efficiency-as-built-p3-task-34) rule 10: "Go, with a pre-review to be sure of what you do". `security-reviewer` and `constraints-hunter` reviewed the design before any code; their findings and what was applied are in the [review record's Pre-review](../../reviews/code/P3-fu53.md#pre-review). Branch `autopilot/P3-fu53`.

- **`sacct` lines are bound to the job's directory.** `parse_sacct` takes, per job ID, the directories the job may have been launched from, with the run's launch time. A job line counts only when its WorkDir, normalised and never resolved, is one of them and its `Submit` is not more than 300 s before the launch. Other lines are dropped and no longer remove the job's row (SEC-P3T34-04).
- **A resized job reads `available`.** Its older RESIZING records are left out. The final record is the row, with CPU and memory None, since the steps under the RESIZING record are not summed. Two final records in one directory still give no row.
- **Steps join the job line just before them**, with the same base ID, and nothing else.
- **Each call has its own 16-character delimiter** and `TZ=UTC`; a line that does not split into the 17 fields is dropped, so a newline in a WorkDir cannot forge a line.
- **The server side.** The expected directories are the job's path and its `realpath`, computed in the file pool when the job is first asked. A job with no directory is never asked. A run that asks for a job while a call for it is in flight is queued again, with no try counted.

Not run here: the `slurm-mini` cases, which need P1 Task 22 and a Docker daemon (FU-P3-60): a resized job, a requeued job, a runs root behind a symlink, a newline in a WorkDir, a 16-character delimiter, and `TZ=UTC` on `Submit`. The choices the design left open are [inbox #119](../../reviews/inbox.md#119-assumed-sacct-rows-bound-to-the-runs-workdir-as-built-fu-p3-53), assumed; its options (b) are FU-P3-61.
