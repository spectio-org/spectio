## What the step does

- **Step 0** (`refuse_script`, `api/results.py`; called first by `result_file` and `workdir_raw`). `Sec-Fetch-Dest` of `script`, `worker`, `sharedworker` or `serviceworker` is `422 file-not-allowed` ("not served to a script or a worker") on both raw routes, whatever `as`.
- **Step 9** (`_imaging`, `_Slot`, `_Held`; `app.py` creates `app.state.imaging_slots`, a `BoundedSemaphore(IMAGING_SLOTS = 3)`). An imaging read takes a slot without waiting, or is `503 runner-offline` ("busy reading images") at once. The slot is taken before the pool.
    - **When it comes back.** A `_Slot` counts its holders: the response, and every pool job it started (`open_raw`, each chunk read), through `slot.take()` passed as `on_done`. The semaphore is released when the count reaches 0, so a read stuck on a stalled mount keeps its slot after the request stopped waiting (SEC-P3FU102B-R2-01).
    - **`FilePool.run(fn, timeout_s, on_done=None)`** (`files/pool.py`). The worker calls `on_done` in its `finally`, which also covers a job cancelled while queued; a pool that refuses a job at once calls it before it raises `Stalled`. Either way, once. `in_pool` (`api/taskfiles.py`) passes it on.
    - **The response** (`_Held`) drops its reference in `__call__`'s `finally` (end, failure, client gone: CR-P3FU102B-01) and in `__del__` (a response never sent); the `_imaging` error path drops it too.
- **One decision for the cap and the dispatch** (`raw_response`). `as=tiff` or `as=zarr`, not `download`, `Sec-Fetch-Dest: empty` goes to `_imaging`; every other read has the 1 GiB cap. The TIFF name and Zarr key checks (steps 4 and 5) now run before the attachment of step 6 (closes SEC-P3FU102A-01 and CR-P3FU102A-01 of [P3-fu102a](P3-fu102a.md#findings)). Two `Range` header fields are refused with `422` on `Range` (closes SEC-P3FU102A-02).
- **The CSP** (`static.py`). `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'` and `worker-src 'self'`, on every screen; no `'unsafe-eval'`, no `blob:`. `test_web.py` pins the header.
- **Tests** (`tests/api/test_imaging.py`, 23 in the file, 8 new): step 0 on both routes and all four destinations; the slots (`test_req14_imaging_read_slots`); a slot back when the client goes away (`..._slot_back_on_client_gone`); a slot held while the mount stalls (`..._slot_held_while_stalled`); a slot back on abort and on a later refusal; session and cross-site on both routes and both `as` values.
