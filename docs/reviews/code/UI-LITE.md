# UX review · the 0.0.1 lite boards

`ux-reviewer` on the 0.0.1 lite boards of `design/ui-canvas/` (commit `0b89cb9`, page **0.0.1 lite** of `canvas.json`), 2026-10-28, against [`ui-spec.md`](../../specs/ui-spec.md), [`mvp-0.0.1.md`](../../specs/mvp-0.0.1.md), [`api-and-data-model.md`](../../specs/api-and-data-model.md) and [P1](../../plans/P1-walking-skeleton.md) Tasks 17 to 20 and 25. The findings were applied on branch `autopilot/ui-lite-review` with the decisions below. Thomas had asked for a home / overview screen and for a run detail "with all its steps and their status" (2026-10-28). Board table: [`design/README.md`](https://github.com/weber8thomas/spectio/blob/main/design/README.md#001-lite-boards). Format: [`README.md`](../README.md).

| Id | Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| UX-292 | major | the boards showed named stage cards, "stage n of m" and a Stage column, which the 0.0.1 API cannot serve | applied with Thomas's decision of 2026-10-28 ("les 2 dans 0.0.1": both): `LiteRunDetail`, `LiteRunDetailSnakemake` (new), `MLiteRunDetail` show the stages in order, declared or detected (see the stage-detection spike, pending), each with a state word and icon, "n of m tasks" and a Declared or Detected badge, opening to its processes or rules in first-seen order, plus "Not in a stage"; the detected examples (nf-core/rnaseq subworkflows; a MosaiCatcher-like run with one stage per rule file) are marked [to be validated] by that spike; with no stage, `LiteRunFailed`, `LiteRunObserved` and `MLiteRunObserved` show the flat list and "No stages for this pipeline"; "stage n of m" stays in the run header of runs that have stages; the Stage column and stage text on home and the runs list are gone; [P1](../../plans/P1-walking-skeleton.md) Assumption 6 and Task 18 (`GET /runs/{id}/stages`) |
| UX-293 | major | "Refreshed every 5 s" and "poll every 5 s" contradicted the SSE decision | applied: every screen says "live, updated N s ago"; the `LiteLegend` caption rewritten for SSE (one stream per browser, the age of the last frame, "Reconnecting…"); MLiteRuns footer says the list follows the server's event stream |
| UX-294 | major | runs started from a terminal were not drawn | applied: neutral "From a terminal" chip in `LiteRuns`, `MLiteRuns`, `LiteHome`, `MLiteHome`; new boards `LiteRunObserved` and `MLiteRunObserved` with no Cancel, "Started from a terminal · monitor only in 0.0.1", Head "head on login2, not checked", no tmux card, the Logs tab showing the engine log only with the reason ([architecture §13.8](../../specs/architecture-plugins-and-packaging.md#138-runs-started-from-a-terminal)) |

| UX-296 | major | the runs list filters, the counts and other facts needed endpoints Task 18 did not define | applied: P1 Task 18 gains `GET /runs?state=&q=`, `GET /runs:summary?group_by=state` with no time window ("last 24 h" dropped), `origin`, `engine_version` (from `check_engine` at launch, from the pointer for terminal runs) and `workflow.version` (`null` when undeclared; Nextflow `workflow.manifest.version` **[to be validated]**), and a minimal `GET /version` → `{version, node, runs_root, account}`, with tests; partition and every other unserved fact dropped from the boards; [api §5](../../specs/api-and-data-model.md#5-endpoint-catalogue-api-v1) dated note 2026-10-28 for the subset it already specifies; the 0.0.1 `/version` shape stays in P1 |

| UX-298 | minor | Home is a fourth screen while the specs said three | applied: Home kept (Thomas, [decision log](../../decisions/log.md#2026-10-28-001-has-four-screens-home-kept)); [mvp-0.0.1 §1](../../specs/mvp-0.0.1.md) and [dev-env §15.1](../../specs/dev-environment-and-testing.md#151-capabilities-by-version) say four screens; P1 M4 and Tasks 19 and 20: routes `/`, `/runs`, `/launch`, `/runs/:id`, Task 20 depends on `runs:summary` |
| UX-299 | major | loading, empty, not-found and refused states were missing for the three screens | applied: new board `LiteStatesRun` (run detail loading, not found, queued with head not started, launch loading, directory refused, value refused after submit, no tasks yet, no match, log not readable, log not created yet); `LiteStates` gains "Home, first use" |
| UX-300 | minor | phone gaps: no Tasks or Logs board, small chips, unwrapped meta lines, no sticky Launch | applied: new `MLiteRunTasks` and `MLiteRunLogs`; 44 px chips with a fade at the scroll edge; ellipsis on the meta lines of `MLiteHome` and `MLiteRuns`; `MLiteLaunch` gains the "Alignment options" group with the two changed values and a sticky Launch bar above the tab bar |

| UX-303 | minor | naming and copy drift | applied: `spectio-open embl` in the expired-link state, the P1 Review Focus 5 wording, "Download last 64 KiB", one session-name form `spectio-<run ID>`, accessible names on the filter buttons (`"Failed, 3 runs"`) |
