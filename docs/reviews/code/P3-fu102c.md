## What the step does

- **`LiteImageViewer`** (desktop, four variants, each a card):
    - an OME-TIFF with three channels: Z and T ranges, zoom buttons, the keys line, the facts line, and the channel panel beside the image (a switch per channel, Low and High contrast ranges on the rows that are on, the range, Reset contrast);
    - a 40-channel variant: Filter channels, "6 of 40 on", its own scrolling Channels region, the 7th-channel line, and the status line "Spectio stopped reading tiles: 2,000 reads." with Reload;
    - an RGB slide: Reset contrast only, and the status line "Some tiles could not be read";
    - a `.tif` with OME-XML in the flat view, with Open in the image viewer.
- **`MLiteImageViewer`** (phone, 360 and 320 px): the first plane in the flat view, "Open on a larger screen" with Copy link, and the too-large TIFF with its sentence.
- **Names for screen readers.** Switches are `<button role="switch" aria-checked>`; each channel's switch and range inputs name the channel by number and name; the contrast values are shown as text, not as a tooltip.
- **Left for FU-P3-102c2** (named in the commit message of `7ab42767` only, not in the board footnotes): `LiteImageViewerStates` (every state of the imaging note), the three `LiteResults` rows (TIFF, OME-TIFF, OME-Zarr) and the OME-Zarr folder page's Open as an image.
