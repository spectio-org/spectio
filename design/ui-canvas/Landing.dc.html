Checkpoints, not guesswork

Declare stages once in spectio.yml. Spectio stops after each one, shows the QC evidence, and waits for a decision. Change parameters and rerun from that stage, continue to the end, or restart from scratch. The engine cache does the rest.

Built for humans, AI agents and lab robots

One API behind three doors: the web app for people, an MCP server for AI agents, webhooks for instruments and LIMS. Every decision records who made it, and why.

Spectio runs the pipeline, depictio turns its results into interactive dashboards. Same look, same accounts, one click between them.
